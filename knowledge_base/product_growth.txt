Topic: User Retention

Practical retention principles:
- Help users experience the product's core value early.
- Study where users stop using the product.
- Interview users who stop returning.
- Improve onboarding to help users reach their first meaningful outcome.
- Track retention by cohort.

Topic: Product Growth

Practical growth principles:
- Identify the target audience and the problem the product solves.
- Measure activation, engagement, and retention.
- Run small experiments and evaluate their results.
- Prioritize improvements based on user evidence.
